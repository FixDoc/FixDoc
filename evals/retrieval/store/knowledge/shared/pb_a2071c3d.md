## When to use

Cert expiry alerts fire ~2 weeks before AKS node certs lapse.

## Steps

1. az aks rotate-certs on the cluster.
2. Wait for pools to cycle.
3. Confirm nodes Ready and workloads rescheduled.

## Verification

Expiry check shows new not-after dates.
