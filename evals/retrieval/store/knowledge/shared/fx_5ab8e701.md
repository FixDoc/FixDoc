## Symptom

After scaling the nodepool, pods stay Pending; scheduler reports 0/14 nodes are available although nodes show Ready.

## Root cause

Azure CNI reserves max_pods IPs per node; the nodepool subnet has no free addresses.

## Fix

Move the nodepool to a dedicated subnet or lower max_pods; verify availableIpAddressCount.

## Verification

Pods schedule within a minute; subnet has headroom.
